CTP and PS plates

Offset plates on grained, anodised aluminium in 0.15–0.30 mm thickness. Specifications come from the manufacturer and can change, so ask us to confirm before you order.

HD-HL CTCP plate

UV-CTP plate that works with the main violet (405 nm) platesetters and developers. Wide exposure window and fast ink-water balance, so less waste at start-up.

  • 405 nm UV laser
  • 2–98% at 200 lpi
  • 50,000–80,000 impressions unbaked
  • 0.15–0.30 mm
Request a quote

HD-ST CTCP plate

UV-CTP plate with a coating made to resist UV inks. Fast exposure and a longer run length for commercial and packaging work.

  • 405 nm UV laser
  • 1–99% at 200 lpi
  • 80,000–100,000 impressions unbaked
  • 0.15–0.30 mm
Request a quote

HD-DS thermal CTP plate (double layer)

Double-layer thermal plate for long runs. Handles UV ink without baking and tolerates changes in developer strength and temperature.

  • 830 nm thermal laser, 120–140 mJ/cm²
  • 1–99% at 450 lpi or FM screen
  • 150,000–200,000 unbaked, over 500,000 baked
  • Daylight handling
Request a quote

HD-3 thermal CTP plate

Single-layer thermal plate with steady batch-to-batch quality and sharp dots at fine screen rulings.

  • 830 nm thermal laser, 110–130 mJ/cm²
  • 1–99% at 200 lpi
  • 100,000 unbaked, over 300,000 baked
  • Daylight handling

HD-4 thermal CTP plate

Forgiving thermal plate that copes with changing processor conditions. Smooth tones and quick ink roll-up with little dampening.

  • 830 nm thermal laser, 110–130 mJ/cm²
  • 1–99% at 200 lpi
  • 100,000 unbaked, over 300,000 baked
  • Daylight handling
