Flexo printing plates

Photopolymer plates for flexible packaging, labels, corrugated boxes and folding cartons. They hold fine highlight dots, are ready for flat-top dots, transfer ink evenly with solvent and water-based inks, and resist ozone and solvents. Specifications come from the manufacturer and can change, so ask us to confirm before you order.
